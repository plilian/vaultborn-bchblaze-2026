How to Play

Build a lasting kingdom on a shared frontier. Develop your city, command its armies, and earn your place in the season standings.

Raise a kingdom

Start with a free protected castle. Produce wood, stone, iron, food, and crystal, then spend those resources on buildings, research, and a stronger garrison.

Earn your victories

Train troops and assign a commander. Scout NPC garrisons and take expeditions using earned supplies, or join seasonal PvP to challenge eligible rivals.

Choose your allies

Scout and hold territory for production. Join an alliance, exchange supplies, and negotiate treaties. Earn Season Points through development and strategy, then follow individual and alliance rankings.
