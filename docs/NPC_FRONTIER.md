# NPC kingdoms and expeditions

Named NPC kingdoms use the regular production, warehouse, recruitment, commander, research, and battle rules. Their faction determines which structures, formations, and commanders they prefer. The same resource recipes apply to their orders. A court can change taxation, fund relief, send and intercept scouts, agree to ceasefires, and choose a protectorate response. A vassal with adequate resources and a tolerable power gap can prepare and launch a rebellion. [NPC policies](../packages/game-engine/npc.mjs)

NPCs plan every two game hours while a founded player has acted in the past day. When a realm is idle, its regular storage limits and offline accrual cap still apply. Autonomous border clashes target NPCs and occur at most once every twelve hours. Recently depleted garrisons can schedule reinforcements from their own manpower. Archived season standings exclude NPCs. [Economy scheduler](../packages/game-engine/economy.mjs)

Players can scout and lead expeditions after the tutorial, including during starter protection and with seasonal PvP disabled. An expedition costs 8 wood and 24 food. A city can lead two expeditions in a rolling day, with two hours between its expeditions, twelve between rematches, and a two hour rest period for a challenged NPC garrison. Successful forces transfer goods from the defeated city, subject to its stock, the winner’s carrying and storage capacity, and a cap of 20 units per resource or 3 crystals. The same recorded combat engine calculates casualties. [Expedition eligibility and transfers](../packages/game-engine/npc.mjs)

An envoy costs 12 wood and 16 food. A kingdom can send one envoy every twelve hours. Willing NPC courts accept a twelve hour ceasefire through the treaty system; a court that has just been attacked or a sufficiently strong Crimson war host can decline. Ceasefires protect their signatories from ordinary attacks and expeditions while active. [Treaty records](../packages/game-engine/diplomacy.mjs)

Frontier camps occupy ordinary land on the atlas. A player can conquer a reachable camp from its NPC warden, using the same supplies and limits as a city expedition. One occupied camp per city counts toward its normal territory and alliance capacity and produces half the normal base yield. The occupation ends after 48 hours and returns its surviving garrison to the player's city. Competitive claim escrow, CashTokens, and rare and strategic sites follow their separate existing rules. [Territorial ownership and recovery](../packages/game-engine/territories.mjs)

Run `node scripts/simulate-npc.mjs` to exercise the game engine at 0, 1, 2, 5, 10, 20, 50, 100, 500, and 1,000 active players over eight simulated hours. The script checks conservation of transferred loot and supplies, daily expedition limits, NPC storage caps, idle realms, and the absence of BCH accounting. It reports deployment counts, victories, completed NPC orders, scouting, and ceasefires. [Population simulation](../scripts/simulate-npc.mjs) · [Focused tests](../packages/game-engine/npc.test.mjs)

The fixed-seed run recorded no NPC orders or fights while idle. With one player, it recorded two expeditions, one player victory, and 51 completed NPC orders. With 1,000 players, 209 commanders started 349 expeditions and NPCs completed 498 orders. The per-garrison cooldown still limits expeditions during large bursts of activity. [Population simulation](../scripts/simulate-npc.mjs)
