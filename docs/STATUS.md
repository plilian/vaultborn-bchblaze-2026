# VAULTBORN scope and evidence

> **Current rules (19 November 2026):** the economy, prices and custody described in parts of this document were replaced. The authoritative description is [ECONOMY_DESIGN.md](ECONOMY_DESIGN.md) and the transaction list is [BLOCKCHAIN_TRANSACTION_MATRIX.md](BLOCKCHAIN_TRANSACTION_MATRIX.md). Where this document differs, those two are right.

The [current local game audit](LOCAL_GAME_AUDIT.md) adds versioned checkout and combat rules, recruitable commanders and stat projections, and an independently verifiable BCH/Nostr identity. It ran no transactions on any BCH network and published no relay events. The on-chain evidence below comes from earlier separately authorized work.

VAULTBORN keeps the existing 16-city world, 10 buildings, six troop classes, eight commanders, research, deterministic combat, scouting, sabotage, alliance diplomacy and rebellion lifecycles. Existing SQLite world, sessions, wallets and receipts remain in place. The English-only LTR interface uses reusable in-game menus, a campaign score with saved volume settings, tactical motion, victory and defeat treatments, progress objectives and achievements.

The browser checks cover desktop and mobile play, custom form controls, WizardConnect pairing, the Council and exact checkout review. The local load run served 336 requests across 48 sessions with zero failures. Balance runs include 12,000 baseline battles, 36,000 paired resolutions, 1,000 player histories, 200 political trials and 24,000 additional escrow simulations. [Kingdom simulation data](vaultborn-kingdom-simulation.json)

Public Chipnet confirmed the four-way split and burn, funded City Hall upgrade, paid raid and escrow settlement, sponsor funding, and prize claim. A separate historical browser checkout used the official WizardConnect SDK with a disposable wallet over a loopback relay and obtained a confirmed Chipnet upgrade. That public relay attempt timed out. The current pass pairs the official Selene web release through a documented local transport bridge, without a transaction; public-relay and native mobile interoperability remain unverified. [Wallet and guide verification](WALLET_GUIDE_VERIFICATION.md). BCHN Regtest has also mined territorial and political covenant settlements, both political refund branches, paid political outcome anchors, and immutable charters in prior work. [Chipnet verification](VAULTBORN_CHIPNET.md) · [Political verification](POLITICAL_MECHANICS.md)

The game supports local guest accounts, BCH signed-message recovery, private Nostr recovery, and the existing portable dual-key proof. The wallet and game-guide pass uses local protocol fixtures and browser checks; it sends no BCH transaction. Public relay interoperability, moderation, transferring actual signing authority to a DAO, and Mainnet wallet and observer support remain open. The Chipnet treasury uses an operator signer. The offline Mainnet preflight validates an owner-supplied recipient and amount. Tournament rankings, sponsorship placement, analytics, and council authorization are local application features.

Commander art uses portrait motion and combat sprites. Art and animation are available in the game, and fully jointed commander animation remains open. [Local BLAZE materials](BLAZE_SUBMISSION_LOCAL.md) · [Presentation copy](VAULTBORN_PITCH.md)
