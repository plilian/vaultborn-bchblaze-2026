# Art direction

The reference boards establish a painterly medieval frontier with gold-edged navy panels, warm stonework, emerald home territories, crimson rivals, purple intelligence and resistance, and legible status colors. Original Codex ImageGen illustrations supply the terrain, six stronghold sprites, eight portraits, six unit sprites, six event scenes and the 30 building tiers. The runtime draws territories, cities and units independently of the scenery.

The generation prompts asked for a UI-free isometric river valley with clearings at the gameplay coordinates, six separated fortress silhouettes in faction colors, eight square commander portraits, three architecture tiers in consistent camera perspective, and six isolated troop classes. Event boards depict victory, a besieged Ironhaven, rebellion, protectorate, the season finale, and the vault. `scripts/package-art.py` crops the sheets, removes their chroma backing, and packages WebP textures. The project-art use and dimensions are listed in [the manifest](../packages/assets/manifest.json).

Battle scenes animate painted troop sprites in formations. Charge, impact, collapse, and retreat follow nine server exchanges across 54 seconds. When archived combat can be reproduced, the replay shows actual losses by class in each exchange. Players can pause, scrub, or go straight to the outcome. Reduced motion keeps the same counts and timing without camera shake or moving formations. The original battle score fades in as the frontier score fades out; combat, victory, and defeat have separate cues. Commander art uses short focus and hover movement in the roster.
