# VaultBorn game design document

> Money rules and prices are described in [ECONOMY_DESIGN.md](ECONOMY_DESIGN.md), and every transaction the game causes in [BLOCKCHAIN_TRANSACTION_MATRIX.md](BLOCKCHAIN_TRANSACTION_MATRIX.md). The game runs on Bitcoin Cash Mainnet at [vaultborn.parhamlilian.com](https://vaultborn.parhamlilian.com).

**Build • Defend • Conquer**

VaultBorn (first developed as BCH Dominion) is a persistent browser strategy game. Commanders build a city, train a garrison, scout and contest a medieval frontier, and compete for seven-day season standing. The interface uses English and reads left to right. Most commands use earned resources. Optional Bitcoin Cash payments, signed in the player's own wallet, pay for rushes and premium improvements, paid intelligence, political operations, War Chest deposits, matched-stake raids and territory stakes; sponsors fund the season prizes. See the [current progression and stat rules](PROGRESSION_AND_STATS.md), [territory protocol](TERRITORY_PROTOCOL.md), and [offline audit](LOCAL_GAME_AUDIT.md).

## Campaign loop

1. Found a settlement and choose a banner. Everything is open from the first day and every kingdom is in the season from the start; an optional campaign guide walks through the first steps and pays a reward at the end.
2. Collect the civic levy, plan a construction project and assign a training queue. The city generates wood, stone, iron, food and crystals. Levels and upgrades persist through refresh and season changes.
3. Explore the frontier, select a target, inspect its public muster estimate and send reconnaissance. Protect the home city with a watchtower operation.
4. Choose a commander, tactic and formation. Raid a city that is at most 20% weaker than yours, or any stronger one, or hold a landmark for production and season contribution.
5. Use an alliance to trade aid, share limited intel, rally around a target and negotiate. A strong alliance sponsor can fund a temporary protectorate offer. The receiving city can refuse, contest, negotiate or accept, then organize independence.
6. Finish a season and review the archived standings. The top five share the season prize pool, paid from its contract with the attestors' signatures.

Building, recruitment, research, free raids, spying and rebellion work without a wallet. No payment leaves the player's wallet without the player's approval in that wallet.

## World and frontier

Players' cities share the frontier with five named NPC kingdoms; the local preview adds seeded demo kingdoms and alliances. The rendered world includes highlands, coast, river crossings, forests, plains and drylands. Banners distinguish Emerald Dominion, Azure Order, Crimson Legion, Free Cities and the neutral Imperial Concord. Map selection links to the city inspector, battle preflight, target intel, territorial claims and map marches generated by real combat events.

The original landmarks and seeded city coordinates remain part of the Emerald Frontier. A newcomer receives one free Starter Castle and a matching homeland with no upkeep or extra production. Sixteen reserved core clearings are checked against saved settlements and landmarks. Earlier saves keep their established coordinates, including any close placements created before the checks existed.

When all usable plots are occupied, the next adjacent region opens with sixteen new plots, an NPC watch, and a nearby camp. Ordinary claim sites open when the region reaches 1, 6, and 11 residents who have founded their city. The atlas adds no Strategic or Rare sites. Each tile has a saved identifier and neighbors, with locations measured in the same units as the existing supply rules. Eligible paid contests, garrisons, and conquest use the existing [territory rules](TERRITORY_PROTOCOL.md). Temporary NPC camp expeditions use earned resources.

Run `node scripts/simulate-frontier.mjs` to reproduce one-day scenarios at 0, 1, 2, 5, 10, 20, 50, 100, 500 and 1,000 active players. Run `node scripts/load-test.mjs --verify-restart` for a local 64-session burst with gameplay sampling and a SQLite restart. The flag `--clients=1000 --registrations-only` runs a queued HTTP registration burst using 64 concurrent sockets. These commands do not connect to a blockchain provider.

## City and development

| Building | Standard effect | Selected BCH improvement |
|---|---|---|
| City Hall | City maturity and maximum support-building level | Founder's civic entitlement |
| Barracks | Garrison capacity and recruitment timing | General rush route |
| Wall Fortress | Defense and resilience | General rush route |
| Vault Treasury | Earned commodity storage | Gold-trimmed treasury entitlement |
| Workshop | Construction speed and siege unlock | General rush route |
| Marketplace | Quote efficiency for earned-resource exchange | General rush route |
| Research Library | Tiered Logistics, Fortification, War Doctrine and Signalcraft | General rush route |
| Alliance Hall | Aid allowance and rally access | Gold-trimmed pennant entitlement |
| Scout Tower | Scouting effectiveness and counterintelligence | Additional private intel archive slot and ranger entitlement |
| Farm & Mine | Passive production for all five commodities | General rush route |

Buildings have persistent levels 1–9 and a standard resource recipe. The art library has three modular isometric tiers. Building, research and training use separate timed queues. The Home City detail previews levels, costs, timing requirements and availability. Construction, research and training are open from the first day. Iron, food and crystal costs create choices between troops, science and development.

Rush halves the remaining time of a construction in progress, once per construction. It applies after one confirmation when the construction has less than an hour left at checkout, otherwise after two. If the construction has finished by then, the rush is owed as a service credit for the next one. Paid specials and rush share a one-use rolling daily cap. The inventory shows city entitlements and credits. The economy coefficients and measured progression are documented separately. [Economy design](ECONOMY_DESIGN.md)

## Army and battle

Units are Infantry, Shield Infantry, Archer, Cavalry, Siege Unit and War Mage. Recruit available troops in orders of 1–30. The standard path unlocks siege and mages from Workshop 2 and Research Library 2. The counter matrix favors shields against horses, cavalry against archers, and siege against shields and defensive works.

Eight commanders trade local advantages. Warden King protects early casualties. Castle Engineer shortens build time. Vault Keeper raises earned-resource storage. Battle Commander adds opening pressure. Scout Ranger improves mission odds. Rebel Queen lifts mobilization morale. Merchant Prince improves exchanges. Oracle Scholar lowers research cost. The roster is available through ordinary play and a commander occupies one active slot.

Use Battle Line for general pressure, Spearhead for infantry and cavalry, or Skirmish Screen for archers and mages. Choose Balanced, Assault, Flank or Siege as tactics. The server compares eligible armies and resolves nine deterministic exchanges, accounting for rosters, lane posture, terrain, walls, heroes, research and fresh intel. Both participants can view the resulting replay and loss report. The looted quantity comes from earned inventory only. Victory can gain one landmark and seasonal contribution. There are no shields this season: raids are limited by three launched and three received per kingdom per day, an alliance budget, and an eight-hour pause between the same two kingdoms. ## Espionage

Basic Recon estimates army size, Deep examines troop mix and formation, Supply estimates resources, and Sabotage can suppress production by 15% for one hour. Decoy deployment feeds misleading reports from the issuing city's own ground. Operations spend earned resources and Scout Points, require an available operative, and have explicit completion timers. Five Scout Points can accumulate, regaining one per 40 minutes.

Tower disparity, Signalcraft, Scout Ranger and active counterintelligence affect seeded success. Detection can cost one operative for 45 minutes and notify the defender. Counterintelligence may raise an unverified sentry alarm. Reports provide a confidence estimate and expire after three hours. A fresh report can be shared once with a current alliance. Decoys and stale intelligence limit the benefit, while a recent report contributes at most a six-percent combat pressure modifier. Reports contain game state only.

## Diplomacy and political control

Alliances retain membership, invitations, rallies, and private chat and scouting. Alliance and federation councils can elect leaders, assign diplomat and quartermaster roles, vote on foreign policy and sanctions, and appropriate resources voluntarily contributed to their own reserves. A federation coordinates its signatories without merging their personal wallets or kingdoms.

The Politics & Diplomacy assembly tracks Legitimacy, Public Loyalty, Diplomatic Influence, and Political Unrest. Tax policy, civic spending, battlefield outcomes, trade pressure, and political events move these indicators and affect production, morale, territory yield, and the ability to negotiate or muster a conflict. Players can propose or counter a versioned peace, ceasefire, non-aggression pact, defense or military assistance agreement, trade agreement, territory sharing, reparations, protectorate, or federation. Partners can move real game resources along authorized trade routes; tariffs, restrictions, embargoes, and strategic blockades apply where the issuer controls the traffic. Smuggling and alternate routes give traders ways to respond.

Coalition wars commit allied troops, equipment, resources, intelligence, and logistics to a three-front campaign. Troops leave the available roster until resolution, and losses belong to their contributing kingdoms. Coalition participation respects attack capacity, protection, compatibility with treaties, and external support cooldowns. See [political mechanics](POLITICAL_MECHANICS.md) for rules, privacy, and receipt verification.

A protectorate may be proposed by a leader with at least 60% city power advantage, at least 20 reputation, 12 Influence and the soft-resource recipe. The target must not hold a recent protectorate immunity. A proposal costs 24,000 satoshis. Once verified, it opens a two-hour response window. The independent defender can accept, refuse, negotiate using Influence, or challenge a bounded expedition under the same tactical simulator. NPC targets can take an automated response. Human timeout expires the proposal.

An active protectorate lasts at most 48 hours and transfers 8% of earned production only. The sponsor pays upkeep every six hours, with an escalating cost and a two-protectorate cap. Sponsor release, elapsed term, insufficient upkeep and successful rebellion end the political relationship. Refusal and settlement grant protection from immediate repeat pressure. The sponsor cannot charge an ordinary wallet or remove access to standard gameplay.

## Rebellion

The vassal prepares at least two cells using earned commodities and Influence. Cells build morale over a 15-minute preparation period and act covertly from the sponsor. Defense partners may contribute resources and support, while the sponsor's reputation and holdings affect political stability. The defender starts a ten-minute mobilization. The sponsor may reinforce, release the city, or let the timer resolve. The engine then runs a bounded tactical battle. The resulting report and political state persist. Success restores independence and adjusts reputation. Failure brings a 12-hour retry lock.

Coups and civil wars put two local factions in contest over the government and, where eligible, a frontier holding. An independence campaign can end a protectorate or restore formerly held land if the normal territory rules permit it. Each campaign opens a preparation window, resolves three replayable fronts, publishes a chronicle, and applies a recovery window. Political events offer alternatives such as civic reform, arbitration, negotiated withdrawal discussions, or a vote.

## Season, treasury and settlement

Seasons last seven days. Contribution combines development, research, defense, raiding, intelligence, territory and diplomacy. Rollover archives individual and alliance standings and resets score and territorial ownership while retaining cities, troop rosters and receipts.

Each player has one BCH balance, the War Chest, funded by half of every service payment and by direct deposits. All of it is at risk under matched-stakes raids and nothing else; it is held by a contract of the player's own script, not by the operator. A separate sponsor contract receives opt-in sponsor funding for season prizes (30% Development, 70% prizes). The full money rules are in [ECONOMY_DESIGN.md](ECONOMY_DESIGN.md).

The wallet stays the player's own. A player connects it with WizardConnect, the wallet signs a proof at connection (it becomes the kingdom's sign-in), and every payment is a transaction the player approves in the wallet. Paid PvP uses matched stakes between War Chests. The Mainnet transactions so far are listed in [MAINNET_TRANSACTIONS.md](MAINNET_TRANSACTIONS.md); the state of every part is in [STATUS.md](STATUS.md).

## Interface scope

The browser includes the illustrated world map, city/buildings, training, army posture, heroes, research, earned marketplace, inventory, intel operations/history, war room, combat replay, politics, alliance roster and treaties, rallies, social dispatches, War Vault, receipts, rankings, profile and onboarding. The mobile map uses an inspector sheet and persistent navigation. Accessibility preferences offer reduced motion and contrast.

The assembly adds an animated influence overlay and cinematic notices for major political outcomes. The [Political Archive](https://vaultborn.parhamlilian.com/verify/politics) is available outside the game session and includes treaty, conflict, escrow, and immutable charter evidence after its disclosure window.
