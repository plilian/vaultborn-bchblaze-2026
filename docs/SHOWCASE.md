# Showcase walkthrough

About 150 seconds on the live game at [vaultborn.parhamlilian.com](https://vaultborn.parhamlilian.com) (Bitcoin Cash Mainnet), with a wallet paired through WizardConnect. Paid steps wait for confirmations: show an already-confirmed receipt if a block has not arrived during the take.

1. **0:00–0:20** Open the world map: your city, the NPC kingdoms, resource sites and the Imperial Palace. Show the resource bar, faction borders and a landmark.
2. **0:20–0:45** Open City Hall and start an upgrade. Open the rush checkout: the price, the four outputs (War Chest, prize pool, Development, burn) and the fee, as the wallet will show them.
3. **0:45–1:15** Scout a rival, choose a tactic and launch a free raid. Play the battle replay and scrub to the engagement and losses.
4. **1:15–1:50** Open the paid raid dialog: the matched stake (from 10,000 sat, offered only when the defender's War Chest covers it), the escrow and what reaches the winner. Show a protectorate offer (24,000 sat) and the Politics screen.
5. **1:50–2:30** Open the War Chest: its balance and what is open to a matched raid. Open the public ledger (`/ledger.html`) with the Mainnet transactions and the settlement signed by the attestors, then the season rankings and prize pool. Close on the world map.
