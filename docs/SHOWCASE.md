# Showcase walkthrough

Allow about 150 seconds for the recorded walkthrough. Use an isolated, pre-funded Chipnet game and real verified receipts. The live broadcast timing depends on Chipnet block production. Record the network or show the already-verified receipt if a block has yet to arrive during the take.

1. **0:00–0:20** Open Riverhold, pan the frontier, and inspect Ironhaven. Show the resource bar, faction borders, landmark and eligibility.
2. **0:20–0:45** Open City Hall and queue a normal upgrade. Show the paid convenience quote, exact CashAddr, amount, and visible network receipt for the prepared Chipnet Hall transaction.
3. **0:45–1:15** Send a scout to Ironhaven. Show the confidence and chosen tactics. Open the paid raid receipt, start the replay, and scrub to the engagement and losses.
4. **1:15–1:50** Switch from Riverhold to Stonevale across the separate demo sessions. Show the 24,000-sat protectorate offer, the paid raid dialog with its matched stake (from 10,000 sats, offered only when the defender's War Chest covers it), the defender response, and rebellion preparation.
5. **1:50–2:30** Open the War Chest page. Show the War Chest balance and what is open to a matched raid, the confirmed sponsor funding, the exact reward receipt, and the ranked leaderboard. Close on the world map.
